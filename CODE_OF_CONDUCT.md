# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all participants.

## Our Pledge

- Respect all team members regardless of background or experience level
- Communicate professionally and constructively
- Be open to feedback and continuous learning
- Support each other in achieving project goals
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information
- Report any issues or concerns to mentors immediately

## Expected Behavior

- Be punctual and reliable
- Contribute meaningfully to discussions and code reviews
- Share knowledge and help teammates
- Write clean, well-documented code
- Follow established coding standards and guidelines
- Take ownership of your work and mistakes

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or disrespect of any kind
- Plagiarism or code theft
- Insubordination or defiance of project rules
- Sharing of sensitive project information
- Intentional sabotage of team efforts

---

For violations or concerns, please contact the mentors immediately.
